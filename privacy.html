ytpipe Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

ytpipe is a private tool that publishes videos to the Curionaut YouTube channel. It is used only by the channel owner. No one else signs in to it, and it does not collect information about anyone else.

Google data ytpipe can access

When the channel owner signs in with Google, ytpipe asks for these permissions:

  • youtube.upload: upload videos to the channel.
  • youtube.force-ssl: manage the channel's own videos, thumbnails, captions and playlists.
  • youtube.readonly: read the channel's own videos and their status.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: read the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports.

How the data is used

ytpipe uses this access only to upload and schedule the channel's own videos, add their thumbnails, captions and playlist entries, check their status, and show the owner how the channel's videos perform. YouTube Analytics figures are aggregated by YouTube and do not identify viewers. ytpipe does not read viewers' personal information.

Storage and sharing

  • The Google sign-in token and the analytics figures are stored only on the owner's own computer. Encrypted backups of that computer's data may be kept in the owner's private backup storage.
  • This data is not sold, shared with or transferred to anyone else, and it is not used to train AI models.
  • Stored statistics are kept no longer than YouTube's API policies allow. They are deleted when a video is removed from YouTube, or when access is revoked and not granted again within 30 days.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that, ytpipe can no longer reach the channel.

YouTube API Services

ytpipe uses YouTube API Services. Using it means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
