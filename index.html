ytpipe

ytpipe is the private publishing tool behind the Curionaut YouTube channel. It prepares the channel's own videos, uploads and schedules them, adds their thumbnails, captions and playlist entries, and reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports.

It is not a public service. The only Google account that signs in to it is the channel owner's, and it runs on the owner's own computer.

Privacy policy

ytpipe uses YouTube API Services.
